Forge

About Forge

Forge is a strength-training app with a twist: you log your workouts, and a companion creature grows alongside you as your lifting adds up.

The idea is simple. Consistency is the hard part of training, and a number on a chart is a weak motivator. So in Forge, every workout you log feeds a companion that hatches, grows, and changes character as you keep showing up. Your progress becomes something you can watch, not just measure.

Who makes it

Forge is an independent app built by Judy Leach in Ontario, Canada. It’s a small project, built and maintained by one person.

How it works

You sign in with your Apple ID, log workouts set by set, and the app tracks your training volume and streaks. Your companion’s stage reflects your lifetime total; its demeanour reflects how often you’ve trained recently. There are no ads and no third-party tracking — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what Forge collects.
